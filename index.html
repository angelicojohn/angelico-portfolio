Questions

Questions, answered.

How much does a website cost?

Custom Websites start at $1,500 USD, Growth Websites at $3,000 and custom digital solutions at $5,000. If you’d rather pay monthly, 12-month website subscriptions start at $199/month plus a setup fee. These are starting prices: you get a clear proposal based on your scope before any work begins.

Why are your websites more cost-effective?

AI-assisted tools take care of a lot of the repetitive production work, so more of the hours you pay for go into design, customisation and what your business actually needs.

Are your websites custom-designed?

Yes. Each project is designed around your business, brand, audience and goals.

How long does a website take?

A smaller website usually takes 1-2 weeks. Bigger projects can take 2-4 weeks or longer, depending on scope, how quickly feedback comes back and whether the content is ready.

Do I need a monthly subscription?

No. Website development is available as a one-time project. Monthly website subscriptions, and the care, growth and automation plans, are optional.

Can you improve my existing website?

Yes. Not every business needs a complete redesign. I can improve performance, conversion paths, SEO foundations, integrations and existing functionality.

Do I own my website?

Ownership and handover terms are set out in the project agreement. Upon full payment, you receive the agreed deliverables and ownership rights. Third-party services remain subject to their own licences and terms.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. I work remotely with businesses across different countries and time zones, and pricing is in USD.

Why do you build with Astro?

Astro sends plain HTML and only adds JavaScript where it’s needed, so pages load fast. The code is yours, it can be hosted almost anywhere without a per-site builder plan, and it connects easily to the tools your business uses.

Is my website built by AI?

No. I use AI tools to speed up coding, debugging and repetitive tasks. The design decisions, implementation review, testing and final delivery are mine, and your feedback shapes the result.

Can I update the website myself?

Yes. Everyday content like text, images, services, pricing and blog posts is edited in Sanity Studio, a visual editor set up around your content. Bigger structural changes are available as optional support.

What happens to my SEO if I move my site?

It comes with you. URLs stay the same wherever possible, every changed URL gets a 301 redirect, and titles, descriptions, alt text and structured data are checked before launch.

What is AEO, and do I need it?

Answer Engine Optimisation helps AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google’s AI Overviews understand and cite your business. It builds on good SEO, and its foundations are included from the Growth Website up.

Do you work with agencies?

Yes. I’ve spent years building with agencies: working from approved designs, handling responsive development, CMS and QA independently, and supporting websites after launch.
